English

Hi! Ben Kimim? ("Who Am I?") is a Turkish-language personality quiz app made just for fun.

FAQ

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-in or account; you can type your first name if you like, and it stays on your phone.

Where are my results stored?

Only on your phone. They are never sent anywhere.

Why is the Character Report locked?

It unlocks after you complete at least 3 different quizzes.

How do I change my name or delete my data?

Change your name in Settings > "Adın" (Your name). To delete everything: Settings > "Verilerimi Sil" (Delete My Data).

Are the results real?

No, they are for fun only and are not scientific.
