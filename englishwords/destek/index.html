Hi! English Words for Kids is an English vocabulary app with pictures and sounds for children aged 7–12. It includes about 5,000 words.

FAQ

There is no sound. What should I do?

Turn off the phone's silent switch and turn the volume up.

Does it need the internet?

No. The app works offline.

Is it paid?

No. It is completely free, with no ads and no purchases.

How do I turn off reminders?

Settings > Notifications > English Words for Kids.
